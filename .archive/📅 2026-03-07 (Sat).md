## 1. The Best "Prompt-Based" Alternative: CapCut

If you want to "prompt" a video into existence, **CapCut** (available on the Play Store) is the industry standard for this.

- **The "AutoCut" Feature:** You upload your long video and photos, and instead of just guessing, it gives you dozens of "Templates."
    
- **Why it's better for you:** You can tap on the "6:42" segment of your video within the template and specifically tell the app to "Replace" that part of the edit with your vocal entrance. It handles the music and transitions automatically.

## 3. The "Video Boost" & "Magic Editor" Route

Since you have a **Pixel Fold**, you have hardware-specific tools that other phones don't:

- **Magic Editor (in Photos):** If your photos from the concert are a bit dark or have a random person's head in the way, use this first. It uses the same tech behind "Nano Banana" to clean up the stills before you put them in the video.
    
- **Audio Magic Eraser:** If the crowd near you was screaming too loud during the 6:42 vocal entrance, open the video in the Photos app, hit **Edit > Audio > Audio Eraser**. You can actually turn down the "Noise" (the crowd) and turn up the "Voice" (the singer).
