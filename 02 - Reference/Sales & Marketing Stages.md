#stages

PROSPECTING → QUALIFYING → PRESENTING → NEGOTIATING → CLOSING → ONBOARDING

LEAD → MQL → SQL → OPPORTUNITY → PROPOSAL → NEGOTIATION → CLOSED WON/


**LEAD** *(Initial Contact)*
- New Lead
- Attempted Contact (1st, 2nd, 3rd)
- LVM
- LVE
- Nurture

**MQL - Marketing Qualified Lead** *(Engaged but not sales-ready)*
- Engaged with Content
- Demo Request
- Contact Scheduled

**SQL - Sales Qualified Lead** *(Sales-ready)*
- Discovery Call Held
- Need Confirmed
- Budget Confirmed
- Decision Maker Identified

**OPPORTUNITY** *(Active Deal)*
- Solution Proposed
- Demo Completed
- Evaluation Stage
- Champion Identified

**PROPOSAL**
- Proposal Sent
- Proposal Reviewed
- Questions/Objections
- Revisions Requested

**NEGOTIATION**
- Terms Discussion
- Legal Review
- Pricing Negotiation
- Approval Chain

**CLOSED**
- Won - Contract Signed
- Won - Payment Received
- Lost - No Budget
- Lost - Timing
- Lost - Competitor
- Lost - No Decision
