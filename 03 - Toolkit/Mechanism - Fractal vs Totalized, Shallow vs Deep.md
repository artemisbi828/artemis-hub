## 1. Time‑Horizon Thinking (Beyond Short‑Term Gain)

Use these when contrasting “immediate benefit” with long‑run consequences:

- **Long‑horizon thinking**
- **Longitudinal thinking**
- **Intertemporal reasoning**
- **Lifecycle‑aware**
- **Future‑proofed** / **Future‑resilient**
- **Durability‑oriented**
- **Second‑ and third‑order effects**
- **Deferred‑cost awareness**

> _Good framing_: “This design optimizes for short‑term throughput but ignores second‑ and third‑order effects.”
