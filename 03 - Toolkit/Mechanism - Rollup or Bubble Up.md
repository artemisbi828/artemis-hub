# Bubble-Up

> **“Bubble up” = allow something to surface naturally at a higher level without being forced, over-modeled, or prematurely escalated.**

It implies **upward visibility through aggregation, abstraction, or escalation**.

Lower‑grain data or issues **surface at higher levels** through **rollups, aggregates, or summaries**.

> **“Bubble up” means letting data, issues, or signals surface naturally at higher-level views through aggregation or hierarchy—often intentionally vague, sometimes healthy, sometimes deferring ownership.**

You should mentally ask:

- ✅ _What rollup?_
- ✅ _At what grain?_
- ✅ _With what threshold?_
- ✅ _And who owns it if it does?_

A governance‑mature rephrase you could offer:

> “Let’s define **where** it would surface, and **what signal** would trigger action.”

---
### Examples

- “Let’s let clinic‑level variance **bubble up** to the regional KPI.”
- “Data quality issues will **bubble up** once we aggregate at the month level.”
- “Don’t over‑filter—if it’s real, it’ll **bubble up** in the exec report.”

### What it signals

- Trust in aggregation
- Expectation that **important signals survive rollups**
- Avoidance of premature exclusion

### Semantic translation

- **Upward propagation**
- **Emergent signal via aggregation**
- **Rollup visibility**

Problems are **not immediately escalated**, but allowed to surface when they cross a certain **impact or visibility threshold**.

### Examples

- “We’ll monitor for now—if it persists, it’ll **bubble up**.”
- “Missing mappings will **bubble up** as ‘Unknown’ in reporting.”
- “If adoption drops, it’ll **bubble up** in leadership reviews.”

### What it signals

- Deliberate non-escalation
- Reliance on monitoring or sentinel metrics
- Sometimes a euphemism for “we’re not acting yet”

### Semantic translation

- **Deferred escalation**
- **Threshold-based surfacing**
- **Passive risk exposure**

## 3️⃣ Semantic / Modeling Context (important nuance)

### Meaning

You are **not encoding something directly**, but instead letting it appear **implicitly** through structure or outcomes.

### Examples

- “Let the mapping be simple—edge cases will **bubble up**.”
- “We didn’t model that attribute explicitly; it’ll **bubble up** in usage patterns.”
- “If definitions are unclear, disagreement will **bubble up** at the metric layer.”

### What it signals

- Minimalism in modeling
- Acceptance of ambiguity upfront
- Willingness to refine later

### Governance-adjacent implication

⚠️ Often correlated with:

- **semantic drift**
- **retroactive restatement**
- “We’ll fix it later” risk

---

## 4️⃣ Organizational / Ownership Context

### Meaning

Information, decisions, or concerns **move upward through management layers**—often informally.

### Examples

- “That question will **bubble up** to the steering committee.”
- “We expect this concern to **bubble up** from the business.”
- “If it’s important enough, it’ll **bubble up**.”

### What it signals

- Lack of direct escalation path
- Reliance on hierarchy
- Sometimes avoidance of ownership

---

## 5️⃣ Positive vs Negative Connotations (VERY IMPORTANT)

### ✅ Positive / healthy usage

- “True signals bubble up”
- “Noise falls away; value bubbles up”
- “Let insights bubble up naturally”

Implies:

- Signal > noise
- Stable aggregation logic
- Trust in system design

### ⚠️ Neutral / vague

- “It’ll bubble up later”
- “Let’s see if it bubbles up”

Implies:

- Unclear thresholds
- Passive monitoring

### ❌ Negative / avoidance-coded

- “If it bubbles up, we’ll deal with it”
- “That’s not a priority unless it bubbles up”

Implies:

- Responsibility deferral
- Known risk without ownership

---

## 6️⃣ Precise Alternatives (when you want less ambiguity)

If you want to **tighten language** in docs or meetings:

|Instead of|Use|
|---|---|
|Bubble up|**Propagate upward**|
|Bubble up|**Surface via aggregation**|
|Bubble up|**Escalate once threshold is crossed**|
|Bubble up|**Emerge at higher grain**|
|Bubble up|**Roll up into executive view**|
