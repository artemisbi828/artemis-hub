**Semantic Conflation** -- collapse n conflicting things together 
- **Semantically Overloaded** -- intentionally making one term to represent several concepts (despite warning)
- **False Equivalence**: different name, mis-assumed same meaning --> ❌ FALSE

**Semantic Collision** -- conflict exists due to same-name, diff-concepts (umbrella term)
- SOME conflict (false) 
- SOME similar (true)
- overlap + conflict --> **Semantic Ambiguity**

**Semantic Duplication** -- same concept, represented twice --> ❌ MERGE IT! 
- **Semantic Divergence** -- a synonym that gradually became different due to drift
- **Semantic Drift** -- meaning changed over time
- **Semantic Fragmentation** -- one logical concept unnecessarily represented as multiple concepts

#### Progression
```
AMBIGUITY
One name could mean A or B
        ↓

COLLISION
We discover the name is actually being used
for both A and B
        ↓

FALSE EQUIVALENCE
Someone treats A and B as interchangeable
because of that ambiguity/collision
```
