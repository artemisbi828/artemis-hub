Leap year rule:

- Divisible by 4 → leap year
- Except divisible by 100 → not a leap year
- Except divisible by 400 → leap year

Examples:

|Year|Leap Year?|Days|
|---|---|---|
|2024|Yes|366|
|2025|No|365|
|2026|No|365|
|2027|No|365|
|2028|Yes|366|

Since:

2026 % 4 = 2

2026 is **not divisible by 4**, therefore **365 days**.
