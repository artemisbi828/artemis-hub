---
definition: Harassment is unwelcome conduct that is based on race, religion, color, sex (including pregnancy), gender identity/expression, sexual orientation, marital status, citizenship, national origin, age (40 or older), disability or genetic information, or any other legally protected basis, in accordance with applicable laws. Depending on the circumstances, harassment may include conduct such as stereotyped or demeaning remarks or gestures, bullying, or the display or circulation, whether in writing or electronically, of offensive materials or pictures.
