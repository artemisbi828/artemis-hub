## **Heuristics**

**Definition:**  
**Heuristics** are *practical rules of thumb* or *experience‑based shortcuts* used to make decisions or solve problems **when an exact method is impractical, unknown, or too costly**.

They favor:

*   speed over perfection
*   “good enough” over optimal
*   judgment over proof

**Key idea:** *Helpful, but not guaranteed to be correct.*

### Examples

*   **BI / analytics:**  
    “If row counts jump 10× after a join, it’s probably a bad join.”
*   **Data quality:**  
    “NULLs in a required field usually indicate upstream pipeline failure.”
*   **UX:**  
    “Users won’t scroll past the first dashboard page.”

### Common phrases

*   “Using a heuristic…”
*   “As a heuristic…”
*   “Heuristic approach”

### What heuristics are *not*

*   Not formal algorithms
*   Not mathematically provable
*   Not universally correct

**One‑line summary:**

> *A heuristic is a useful shortcut based on experience, not a guarantee.*
