## **Single-Hierarchy**
- One **ontological parent**
- Can be _used_, _referenced_, or _analyzed_ in multiple contexts
- Example:  
    `new_patient_exam` **is an** `exam`, even though it participates in scheduling, finance, and outcomes.

## **Poly-Hierarchy**
- Has **multiple equally valid “is‑a” parents**
- Removing one parent loses meaning
- Very rare in operational data models
