# Use‑Case Cards (Production)

## Use Case 1 — Orthodontics: No‑Show Reduction
### Problem
High missed appointments drain capacity and drive patient churn. Front‑desk staff spend time chasing no‑shows. Underutilized treatment chairs cost thousands per month.

### Approach
- Deploy SlideSMS with 24‑hour and 2‑hour reminders.
- Build a capacity vs. scheduled dashboard showing utilization by doctor and chair.
- Add front‑desk leaderboards to motivate appointment confirmations.

### Outcome
- No‑shows ↓ 25–35%.
- Chair utilization ↑ 12–18%.
- Staff overtime reduced by 18% (less scrambling to backfill).
- Patient satisfaction ↑ (clear communication).

### Metrics
- Before: 180 scheduled; 45 no‑shows (25%).
- After: 180 scheduled; 15 no‑shows (8%).
- Monthly revenue impact: +$12K–$18K (recovered production).

### Next Steps
- Week 1: Pilot SMS with 2 locations.
- Week 3: Roll out capacity dashboard.
- Week 4: Launch leaderboards + friendly competition.
- Months 2–3: Tune thresholds; add patient communication integrations (Cloud9/OrthoFi).

## Use Case 2 — Tax Firm: Shoebox to Structured
### Problem
SMB clients provide disorganized PDFs, scanned receipts, and handwritten notes. Tax preparers spend 40–50% of billable time on data entry and cleanup—not consulting. Write‑offs are high; capacity is capped.

### Approach
- Deploy Doc‑Scraper to automate W2, 1099, K‑1, and receipt digitization.
- Build exceptions log so staff focus on true edge cases (handwriting, ambiguities).
- Create a pre‑intake form so clients upload before their appointment (reduce rework).
- Standardize data into a "client brief" CSV ready for your system.

### Outcome
- Prep time per client ↓ ~50%.
- Write‑offs ↓ 30% (less rework).
- Capacity ↑ 20 additional returns per season without hiring new staff.
- Client satisfaction ↑ (faster turnaround; fewer "I need that receipt again" emails).

### Metrics
- Before: 150 hours/month on data entry; 10% write‑off rate.
- After: 75 hours/month; 7% write‑off rate.
- Freed hours → advisory services (higher margins).

### Next Steps
- Week 1: Pilot with 10 existing clients; collect sample documents.
- Week 2: Fine‑tune field mapping and exceptions log.
- Week 3: Roll out pre‑intake forms.
- Months 2–3: Track time savings; plan advisory upsell (tax planning dashboards).

## Use Case 3 — Manufacturing: Post‑ERP Ops Signals
### Problem
After migrating from legacy IBM DB2 to Infor CSI (cloud ERP), the operations team lost cross‑functional visibility. Production delays increased. Accounting, Receiving, and Purchasing don't share signals.

### Approach
- Build ops signals dashboard bridging Accounting (GL/AP), Receiving (POs/receipts), and Purchasing (vendor terms).
- Create exception alerts for stockouts, overstock, and delayed receipts.
- Add real‑time production floor KPIs (throughput, cycle time, WIP).
- Train leadership on dashboard; empower teams with daily huddle reports.

### Outcome
- Production delays ↓ 22%.
- Stockouts ↓ 15% (faster reorder visibility).
- Vendor term utilization ↑ 10% (AP dispersion visibility).
- Cash cycle ↓ 5–7 days (better PO timing).

### Metrics
- Before: 8 delayed shipments/month; $450K unplanned expedite costs/year.
- After: 2 delayed shipments/month; $200K expedite costs/year.
- Annual savings: ~$250K + improved on‑time delivery metrics.

### Next Steps
- Week 1: Extract baseline data from Infor CSI; map to dashboard schema.
- Week 2: Build prototype dashboard; validate with Ops/Finance leads.
- Week 3: Deploy to 3–5 key users; collect feedback.
- Weeks 4–6: Roll out to broader team; add SMS alerts for critical exceptions.
- Months 2–3: Integrate supplier APIs (Sysco, US Foods, etc.) for vendor forecasting.
