### Small
- Broil 5-6 inches from top 
- Use scissors to cut to shell down the middle
- Break the ribs underneath and pull meat out to sit on top 
- Broil 10-12 minutes until soft-white but not translucent (finish: pan or microwave blitz 10-15 seconds)

### Large
- Costco Size: Bake 425 8-10 minutes
- Then add butter + **broil**: 2-3 minutes
