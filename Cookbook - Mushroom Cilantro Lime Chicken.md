This dish hits that perfect sweet spot: it feels incredibly indulgent because of the rich, velvety sauce, but the lime juice and a mountain of fresh cilantro keep it bright so it doesn't feel heavy.
Here is how to bring it together in one skillet.
## Ingredients
### The Base
 * **1.5 lbs** Chicken breasts (cut into cutlets or pounded to even thickness)
 * **8 oz** Mushrooms (Cremini or button work best), sliced
 * **2 tbsp** Olive oil (divided)
 * **2 tbsp** Butter
### The Aromatics & Liquid
 * **4 cloves** Garlic, minced
 * **1 cup** Chicken broth
 * **½ cup** Heavy cream
 * **1** Large lime (you'll use the juice and about ½ tsp of the zest)
 * **½ cup** Fresh cilantro, finely chopped
## The Method
**Sear the chicken**
*8-10 min*
Season chicken generously with salt and pepper. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil and 1 tbsp butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Sear chicken until golden brown and cooked through (internal temp 165°F), about 4–5 minutes per side. Remove chicken and set aside on a plate.**Sauté the mushrooms**
*5-6 min*
Lower heat to medium. Add the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil and 1 tbsp butter to the same pan. Toss in the sliced mushrooms. Let them brown beautifully, resisting the urge to stir too much at first so they get a nice crust. Add minced garlic in the last 60 seconds so it gets fragrant without burning.**Build the pan sauce**
*4-5 min*
Pour in the chicken broth, using a wooden spoon to scrape up all those delicious browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Bring to a simmer and let it reduce by about half. Turn the heat down to low and stir in the heavy cream and lime zest. Let it simmer gently for 2 minutes until it starts to thicken.**Finish and emulsify**
*2 min, off heat*
Remove the skillet from the burner. Stir in the lime juice and chopped cilantro (adding lime off-heat keeps the flavor bright and prevents the cream from curdling). Nestled the chicken and its juices back into the sauce, spooning it over the top.
> **Pro Tip:** This sauce is a liquid goldmine. Serve this over a bed of jasmine rice, angel hair pasta, or with a crusty piece of bread to mop up every last drop.
