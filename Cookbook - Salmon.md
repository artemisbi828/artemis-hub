### Cooking Methods: Temperatures, Timings & Doneness

Salmon is done when it reaches an internal temperature of **125°F to 130°F (52°C to 54°C)** for medium (tender and flaky) or **135°F to 140°F (57°C to 60°C)** for medium-well.

| Method | Target Heat | Timing (1-inch fillet) | Technique Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Bake** | **400°F (204°C)** | **12–15 mins** | Even, gentle, forgiving. Excellent for rubs, herb crusts, or mayo toppings. Line pan with foil or parchment. |
| **Broil** | **High Broil** (rack 5–6" from element) | **6–9 mins** | Fast, high-heat caramelization. No flipping needed. Watch closely so spices don't char. |
| **Pan-Sear** | **Medium-High** (stainless or cast iron) | **4–5 mins skin down, 2–3 mins flip** | Best for shatteringly crisp skin. Pat skin completely dry, press flat for the first 30 seconds. |

---

#### Approach A: The Mayo-Parmesan Oven Bake (Easiest, Most Moist)

1. **Prep:** Preheat oven to **400°F (204°C)**. Line a baking sheet with foil and oil lightly.
2. **Top:** Pat salmon dry. Season lightly with salt and pepper. Spread a thin layer (approx. 1 to 1.5 tablespoons per fillet) of the **Garlic-Herb Mayo** mix evenly over the top flesh.
3. **Cook:** Bake on the center rack for **11–13 minutes**.
4. **Finish:** Turn broiler to **High** for 60–90 seconds until the mayo top blisters golden-brown.

#### 1. The Mayo-Baked Upgrades (Savory & Rich)

Mayonnaise acts as an insulator that prevents surface moisture from evaporating while locking in fat and fat-soluble aromatics.

* **Garlic, Herb & Parmesan Mayo:**
* **Base:** 2 tbsp mayonnaise + 1 minced garlic clove (or ½ tsp garlic powder) + 1 tbsp grated parmesan.
* **Seasonings:** Black pepper, pinch of salt, ½ tsp dried dill or parsley, squeeze of fresh lemon juice.
* **Best cooked:** **Bake at 400°F for 10–12 minutes**, then flick on the **broiler for 1–2 minutes** to bubble and brown the parmesan top.


* **Dijon-Herb Mayo Crust:**
* **Base:** 2 tbsp mayonnaise + 1 tsp Dijon mustard.
* **Seasonings:** Garlic powder, coarse ground black pepper, pinch of dried thyme or tarragon.
* **Best cooked:** **Bake at 400°F for 12–14 minutes**. The mustard cuts the rich fat of both the salmon and the mayo.

#### Approach B: High-Heat Broil (Fastest Weeknight Method)

1. **Prep:** Adjust the oven rack so the top of the fish sits roughly 5 to 6 inches below the broiler element. Preheat oven to **Broil (High)**.
2. **Season:** Rub salmon with a few drops of olive oil. Season liberally with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and paprika or your chosen dry rub.
3. **Cook:** Place on a foil-lined baking sheet. Broil undisturbed for **6 to 8 minutes** for a standard 1-inch thick fillet. Watch the edges closely around minute 6 to prevent burning.

#### Approach C: Restaurant-Style Crispy Skin Pan-Sear

1. **Prep:** Pat salmon flesh and skin **completely dry** with paper towels (moisture causes sticking and steaming).
2. **Season:** Season flesh with salt, pepper, and garlic powder (keep the skin side seasoned only with fine salt).
3. **Heat:** Set a skillet over **medium-high heat** until very hot; add 1 tablespoon of high-smoke-point oil (avocado or vegetable).
4. **Sear:** Lay the fillet in **skin-side down**, away from you. Immediately press down gently with a flat spatula for 15–20 seconds so the skin makes full contact with the pan.
5. **Turn:** Cook undisturbed for **4 to 5 minutes** until the skin releases naturally and is browned. Flip and cook flesh-side down for **2 to 3 minutes**. Remove immediately to rest.

### Flavor Profiles & Seasoning Blends (Zero Added Sugar)





#### 2. Dry Spice Blends (Ideal for Pan-Searing or Broiling)

*Apply dry rubs with a micro-coat of neutral oil or directly to lightly patted-dry flesh. Avoid heavy rubbing on delicate fish fibers; press gently.*

* **Savory Blackened / Cajun Rub:**
* **Pantry items:** 1 tsp smoked paprika, ½ tsp garlic powder, ½ tsp onion powder, ¼ tsp dried oregano, ¼ tsp dried thyme, ¼ tsp cayenne pepper, ½ tsp kosher salt, ½ tsp coarse black pepper.
* **Profile:** Earthy, woodsy, spicy, robust.
* **Best cooked:** **Pan-sear in a cast-iron skillet** with a touch of high-smoke-point oil (or broil).


* **Mediterranean Lemon-Herb:**
* **Pantry items:** 1 tsp dried oregano, ½ tsp garlic powder, ½ tsp onion powder, ½ tsp dried dill, coarse sea salt, cracked black pepper, finished with fresh lemon juice and zest.
* **Profile:** Bright, savory, aromatic.
* **Best cooked:** **Bake or pan-sear**, basting with a little butter or olive oil at the end.


* **Warm Citrus-Curry Rub:**
* **Pantry items:** 1 tsp yellow curry powder, ½ tsp garlic powder, ½ tsp ground cumin, ½ tsp ground coriander, ½ tsp smoked paprika, fine sea salt, black pepper.
* **Profile:** Deeply aromatic, savory, golden color from turmeric, zero sweetness.
* **Best cooked:** **Pan-sear or bake at 400°F**.
