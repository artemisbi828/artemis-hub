Using a wok is completely fine—in fact, its wide opening makes saucing and tossing much easier. The only constraint is liquid volume: instead of submerging everything in gallons of broth, you will **par-cook your dense ingredients first in batches**, steam the seafood in the aromatics, and build the sauce right in the wok to finish.

---

### Ingredients (Serves 2–3)

**The Boil Base:**

* **1.5 lbs** Snow Crab or King Crab legs (thawed)
* **1 lb** Large shrimp (raw, shell-on/easy-peel)
* **1 lb** Baby red potatoes (halved)
* **2–3 ears** Sweet corn (cut into 2-inch mini coblets)
* **Optional:** Smoked sausage/andouille (sliced into thick rounds)
* **1** Lemon, halved
* **2** Bay leaves
* **2 tbsp** Old Bay seasoning (for the water)

**Crab Du Jour "Special Mix" Sauce:**
*Crab Du Jour's house special blends Cajun seasoning, Garlic Butter, Lemon Pepper, and Old Bay.*

* **2 sticks (1 cup)** Unsalted butter
* **1 whole head** Garlic, finely minced (about 10–12 cloves)
* **1/2 medium** Yellow onion, finely diced
* **2 tbsp** Cajun seasoning (Slap Ya Mama or Zatarain's)
* **1 tbsp** Old Bay seasoning
* **1 tbsp** Lemon pepper seasoning
* **1 tbsp** Paprika (smoked or sweet)
* **1 tbsp** Brown sugar (balances heat and thickens)
* **1–2 tsp** Cayenne pepper (adjust for desired heat level)
* **1 tbsp** Worcestershire sauce
* **Juice of 1/2 lemon**

---

### Step-by-Step Runbook

1. **Par-Boil Dense Items in the Wok:**
Fill the wok about 2/3 full with water. Add lemon halves, bay leaves, and 2 tbsp Old Bay. Bring to a rolling boil over medium-high heat.

* Add halved baby potatoes: boil for **10–12 minutes** until nearly fork-tender.
* Add corn coblets (and sausage if using): boil an additional **5 minutes**.
* Remove everything with a slotted spoon or spider strainer into a large bowl. Reserve 1/2 cup of the spiced cooking liquid, then discard the rest and wipe the wok dry.


2. **Steam Crab and Shrimp:**
Add 1/4 cup of the reserved cooking water back into the wok over medium heat. Place crab clusters and raw shrimp in, cover with a wok lid (or foil), and steam for **4–5 minutes** until shrimp turn pink/opaque and crab is heated through. Transfer to the bowl with potatoes and corn. Wipe wok dry.


3. **Build the House Mixed Butter Sauce:**
Set the wok to medium-low. Melt both sticks of butter. Add the diced onion and sauté for 2 minutes until translucent, then add the minced garlic and sauté for 1 minute until fragrant (do not let it brown).

Whisk in Cajun seasoning, Old Bay, lemon pepper, paprika, brown sugar, cayenne, Worcestershire sauce, and lemon juice. Simmer on low for **2–3 minutes** to emulsify the spices into the butter.


4. **Toss and Serve:**
Toss the cooked crab legs, shrimp, potatoes, corn, and sausage directly into the wok with the simmering sauce. Stir and toss gently with tongs for 1–2 minutes until every crevice is heavily coated.

Pour directly onto butcher paper/sheet pan or into an oven roasting bag for the authentic boil-bag shake.
