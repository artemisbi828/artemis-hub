1. The "Vinegar & Dish Soap" Method
Vinegar is a natural disinfectant and a powerhouse for neutralizing the alkaline salts in sweat that cause odors.

The Mix: Create a solution of 1 part white vinegar to 2 parts warm water, plus a drop of mild dish soap (like Dawn).

The Process: 1.  Dip a soft toothbrush or a microfiber cloth into the solution.
2.  Scrub the inside of the bill and the sweatband gently in circular motions.
3.  Crucial: Use as little water as possible so you don't saturate the internal stiffener of the bill.
4.  Wipe away the suds with a separate cloth dampened only with plain water.

5. The Vodka/Rubbing Alcohol Spritz (For Bacteria)
If the hat isn't visibly "dirty" but just smells, professionals often use alcohol. It kills bacteria on contact and evaporates quickly.

The Mix: Fill a small spray bottle with cheap, unflavored vodka or 70% Isopropyl alcohol.

The Process: Lightly mist the inside bill and sweatband. Don't drench it; just a fine mist will do. As the alcohol evaporates, it takes the odor-causing bacteria with it.

3. The Baking Soda Paste (For Deep Odors)
If the smell is really stubborn, baking soda will absorb the organic compounds causing the stench.

The Mix: Make a thick paste of baking soda and a tiny bit of water.

The Process: Rub the paste onto the inside of the bill and let it sit for 30–60 minutes. Once dry, brush it off with a dry toothbrush or vacuum it away.
