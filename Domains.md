## Business Domain Cheat Sheet

| Industry                | Common Domains                                            | Typical Entities per Domain                                                                                                                              |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Healthcare**          | Clinical, Administrative, Financial, HR, Legal/Compliance | • Clinical: Patient, Appointment, Diagnosis, Treatment<br>• Financial: Claim, Payment, Production, Collections<br>• Admin: Location, Equipment, Schedule |
| **Real Estate**         | Property, Sales, Leasing, Financial, Legal                | • Property: Listing, Building, Unit, Inspection<br>• Sales: Deal, Buyer, Seller, Offer<br>• Leasing: Lease, Tenant, Rent Payment                         |
| **Manufacturing**       | Production, Supply Chain, Quality, Sales, HR              | • Production: WorkOrder, Product, BOM, Equipment<br>• Supply Chain: Supplier, PurchaseOrder, Inventory<br>• Quality: Inspection, Defect, QualityControl  |
| **Tax/Accounting Firm** | Client Services, Financial, Engagement, HR, Operations    | • Services: Client, Return, Engagement, Deliverable<br>• Engagement: Project, Task, TimeEntry, Billing<br>• Financial: Invoice, Payment, Expense         |
| **Retail/E-commerce**   | Product, Sales, Customer, Supply Chain, Marketing         | • Product: SKU, Category, Pricing, Inventory<br>• Sales: Order, Cart, Transaction, Shipment<br>• Customer: Account, Review, Loyalty                      |
| **Financial Services**  | Customer, Account, Transaction, Risk, Compliance          | • Account: Checking, Savings, Loan, Investment<br>• Transaction: Deposit, Withdrawal, Transfer<br>• Risk: CreditScore, Fraud, Exposure                   |
| **SaaS/Software**       | Product, Customer, Subscription, Support, Usage           | • Customer: Account, User, Organization, License<br>• Subscription: Plan, Billing, Renewal, Upgrade<br>• Support: Ticket, Bug, Feature Request           |
| **Hospitality**         | Guest Services, Operations, Food & Beverage, Events, HR   | • Guest: Reservation, CheckIn, Guest, Room<br>• F&B: Menu, Order, Inventory, Supplier<br>• Events: Booking, Event, Venue                                 |
| **Education**           | Academic, Student Services, HR, Financial, Facilities     | • Academic: Course, Enrollment, Grade, Degree<br>• Student: Student, Application, Financial Aid<br>• HR: Faculty, Staff, Department                      |
| **Logistics/Transport** | Fleet, Operations, Customer, Financial, Compliance        | • Fleet: Vehicle, Driver, Route, Maintenance<br>• Operations: Shipment, Delivery, Tracking, Warehouse<br>• Customer: Order, Quote, Contract              |

## Universal Domains (Appear in Most Industries)

| Domain               | Purpose                 | Core Entities                                       |
| -------------------- | ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Financial**        | Money tracking          | Invoice, Payment, Revenue, Expense, GL Account      |
| **Human Resources**  | People management       | Employee, Department, Position, Payroll, Attendance |
| **Legal/Compliance** | Contracts & regulations | Contract, Agreement, Compliance, Audit, Policy      |
| **Operations**       | Day-to-day execution    | Location, Schedule, Resource, Task, Workflow        |
| **Customer/Client**  | External relationships  | Customer, Contact, Communication, Relationship      |
| **Marketing**        | Acquisition & growth    | Campaign, Lead, Conversion, Channel, Analytics      |
