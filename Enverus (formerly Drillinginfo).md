**Energy Informatics (EI):** The core domain, focusing on the intersection of energy industry data and advanced computational analytics.

**Predictive Analytics / AI:** Utilizing "Enverus AI" and "Instant Analyst" to provide generative and predictive insights for asset valuation and market movements.

**Energy Transition / ESG:** Providing data-driven benchmarking for carbon emissions, CCUS (Carbon Capture), and renewable site selection.

# Lines of Business (LOB)

### 1. Intelligence & Analytics (PRISM Platform)

The flagship LOB providing a "single source of truth" for the energy lifecycle.

- **Subsurface & Upstream:** Tools for operators to evaluate well performance, inventory, and drilling locations.
- **M&A & Financial Services:** Enabling banks and PE firms to conduct due diligence and value energy assets (Oil/Gas and Renewables).
- **Intelligence Research (EIR):** Subscription-based access to human-expert analysis and proprietary reports on market trends.

### 2. Trading & Risk (MarketView)

A specialized suite for commodity traders and risk managers.

- **Real-time Data:** Integration of global commodity market data feeds.
- **Curve Management:** Automation of forward curve building and enterprise data management.
- **ETRM Integration:** Software tools designed to complement Enterprise Trading and Risk Management systems.

### 3. Business Automation (OpenInvoice)

The Fintech arm focusing on the energy supply chain.

- **Source-to-Pay:** Automating procurement, field tickets, and invoicing between operators and oilfield service providers.
- **Spend Management:** Provides visibility into capital allocation and ensures "paid on time" compliance for large-scale energy projects.
- **Mineral Management:** Specialized tools for mineral owners to track payments and value portfolios.
    

# Strategic BI Observations

### 4.1 Competitive Positioning

Enverus maintains a dominant "moat" through its proprietary data lake (25+ years of vetted data) and a high-switching-cost environment. By integrating financial workflows (OpenInvoice) with operational data (PRISM), they create a "sticky" ecosystem where data informs spending.

### 4.2 Growth Vectors

- **Energy Transition:** Heavy investment in CCUS and Renewable site selection tools to capture ESG-driven capital.
- **AI Integration:** The launch of "Enverus AI" seeks to reduce the time-to-insight, potentially moving the business toward a "seats + compute" or "premium intelligence" pricing model.
    

### 4.3 Clarifications & Ambiguities

1. **Revenue Concentration:** Public data does not confirm the exact split between the Upstream (Oil & Gas) and Renewables segments.
2. **Transactional Elasticity:** The exact fee structure of OpenInvoice (per-invoice vs. % of spend) is proprietary and varies by contract volume.
3. **M&A Impact:** Recent acquisitions (e.g., Integrity, RatedPower) have likely shifted the employee count and revenue mix toward the "Services" and "Renewables" categories faster than industry benchmarks suggest.
