### Example 1: The "Happy Path" (Taking & Allowing)

_Context: Two partners on a sofa. Partner A wants to experience the physical sensation of Partner B’s hair. The quadrant is **Taking** (A does the action for A’s pleasure) and **Allowing** (B grants access for A’s pleasure)._

  

```
[Phase 1: Calibration]
Partner A internally checks: "I am feeling playful and drawn to sensory touch. No agenda for sex."
Partner B internally checks: "I am tired, relaxed, open to low-energy contact, but no genital touch."

[Phase 2: The Proposal]
Partner A: "I'd love to run my fingers through your hair for my own pleasure for about 5 minutes. Would you be willing to allow that?"
• Action: Running fingers through hair
• Location: Scalp / hair only
• Duration: 5 minutes
• Quadrant: Taking (A) / Allowing (B)

[Phase 3: The Response]
Partner B: "Yes, absolutely. Go for it."

[Phase 4: Containment & Exit]
• Execution: Partner A plays with Partner B's hair.
• Real-Time: Partner B says, "A little gentler on the temples." Partner A adjusts immediately.
• Closure: At ~5 minutes, Partner A stops, pulls hands away, and says, "Thank you, time's up."
• Debrief: Partner B says, "That felt great, thanks." Agreement cleanly ends.
```

### Example 2: The Boundary & Counter-Offer (Giving & Receiving)

_Context: Partner A wants to offer touch to Partner B (**Giving**), but Partner B doesn't want the proposed action and negotiates a different scope._

  

```
[Phase 1: Calibration]
Partner A: Wants to offer care/service.
Partner B: Has a sore lower back, feeling touch-sensitive, doesn't want full-body massage.

[Phase 2: The Proposal]
Partner A: "I'd like to give you a full shoulder and back massage with oil for the next 15 minutes for your relaxation. How does that sound?"
• Action: Firm oil massage
• Location: Back and shoulders
• Duration: 15 minutes
• Quadrant: Giving (A) / Receiving (B)

[Phase 3: The Response (Counter)]
Partner B: "A full oil massage feels like too much stimulation right now. Would you be willing to just place two warm hands firmly on my lower back over my shirt for 5 minutes instead?"
Partner A: Checks internal capacity: "Yes, I can happily do that."

[Phase 4: Containment & Exit]
• Execution: Partner A places warm, static hands on Partner B's lower back.
• Closure: 5 minutes ends. Partner A lifts hands: "That’s 5 minutes."
• Reset: Agreement is finished; neither assumes continuation.
```

### Example 3: Hard Stop / Mid-Stream Revocation (Handling the "No")

_Context: A physical escalation where an initial agreement is revoked mid-action due to an emotional shift or discomfort._

  

```
[Phase 1: Calibration]
Both agree they are open to making out on the bed.
Hard Limits stated upfront: No clothes coming off tonight.

[Phase 2: The Proposal]
Partner B: "May I kiss your neck and collarbone for a few minutes for my pleasure?" (Taking)
Partner A: "Yes, you may." (Allowing)

[Phase 3: The Response]
Mutual consent established. Action begins.

[Phase 4: Containment & Immediate Stop]
• Trigger: Partner B’s hand instinctively wanders down toward Partner A’s waistband.
• Stop Signal: Partner A immediately says, "Pause."
• Execution Freeze: Partner B instantly removes hands, pulls back 6 inches, and breaks contact without debate or pout.
• Boundary Check:
    Partner A: "My waistband is off-limits tonight, and moving there made me freeze up."
    Partner B: "Heard. Thank you for telling me. My mistake for letting my hand wander."
• Debrief & Reset:
    Partner A: "I want to take a break from touch for 10 minutes and just chat."
    Partner B: "Understood. Touching is completely off the table."
```
