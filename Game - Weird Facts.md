Quirky and weird facts are the best kind of rabbit holes. Here are some of the strangest bits of trivia that sound fake but are actually true:

## Animals & Nature

- **Wombat poop is cube-shaped.** They are the only known animal to produce square-shaped droppings, which helps the "bricks" stay in place to mark their territory without rolling away.
    
- **A group of pandas is called an "embarrassment."** Other strange collective nouns include a "business" of ferrets and a "murder" of crows.
    
- **Trees can "scream."** When plants are stressed by drought or physical damage, they emit ultrasonic vibrations. It’s too high-pitched for humans to hear, but some insects and animals might be able to.
    
- **Turritopsis dohrnii is the "Immortal Jellyfish."** When it gets old or sick, it can revert its cells back to their earliest stage and start its life cycle all over again.
    

## History & Geography

- **The "Great Emu War" of 1932.** The Australian military literally declared war on emus because the birds were destroying crops. The emus actually won—they were too fast and resilient for the machine guns of that era.
    
- **Liechtenstein’s army once came home with more men than they started with.** In 1866, they sent 80 soldiers to war. They returned with 81 because they made a "friend" (an Italian officer) along the way.
    
- **Russia has a larger surface area than Pluto.** Pluto has about 16.7 million square kilometers, while Russia clocks in at roughly 17.1 million.

## Food & Objects

- **Cotton candy was co-invented by a dentist.** William Morrison teamed up with a confectioner to create "Fairy Floss" in 1897. Talk about job security!
    
- **The first VCR was the size of a piano.** Released by Ampex in 1956, it used reels of tape and was way too expensive for home use.
    
- **Froot Loops are all the same flavor.** Despite the different colors, every loop is flavored with the same "froot" blend. Your eyes are just playing tricks on your taste buds.
    

## The Human Body

- **You lose about 50 to 100 hairs a day.** Most of them just end up on your brush or the floor without you noticing.
    
- **Your stomach acid is strong enough to dissolve razor blades.** (Please don't test this.) The stomach lining has to constantly renew itself to prevent the acid from digesting the stomach itself.
    
- **Humans are the only animals with chins.** While other animals have lower jaws, only humans have that specific bony prominence at the bottom. Even chimpanzees and gorillas don't have them.
    

> **Fun Fact:** If you ever feel like you're moving too slow, remember that the Earth is spinning at roughly **1,000 miles per hour** at the equator right now!
