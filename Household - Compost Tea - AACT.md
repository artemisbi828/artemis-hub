Leachate --> 1:10 or 1:20 ratio
- do not use on leaves, only roots

5 gallon HD bucket
- 1 gallon dechlorinated water (pinch -- can get away with 20L (4 buckets))
	- drop the pH --> acidic
- 0.25 -> 0.5 worm castings in tea bag (cheesecloth, pantyhose, nylon paint-strainer bag)
- 1-2 t of liquid/powder kelp (or molasses (dissolved in 1 t warm water))
	- excess sugar explodes bacteria --> consumes oxygen faster than pump
	- without bubbler, microbes use oxygen, 4-6 hours they suffucate

Brew for 24-36 hours (65°F–75°F / 18°C–24°C).
- earthy, sweet, smell (forest after rain)

Apply: 
- leaf inoculation (early morning or late evening/sunset) when UV rays are low and not killing microbes
- root inoculation --> root zone to break down organic matter and deliver bio-avaiable nutrients right to root hairs

Actively Aerated Worm Casting Tea (AACT)
- leachate -- unrefined anaerobic bacteria
- microbes, fungi, protozoa
