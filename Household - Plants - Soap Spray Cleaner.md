- 1 quart spray bottle; 1 quart water
- 1 t castille soap
- 2 T alcohol (70% isopropyl)
- 2-3 drops neem / horticulture oil
- shake! until foamy
- targeted-application: apply every 4-6 days, 2-3 weeks to catch eggs. 
	- avoid daylight -- magnifying glass
	- do not use on ferns or fuzzy leaves like african violets --> traps moisure --> rot / fungal leaf spots
- preventative-application: once every month (3-4 weeks)

**Why castille**: vegetable-oil-based free of harsh detergents, synthetic fragrances, degreasers --> doesn't damage plant foliar defenses

**Why alcohol**: breaks waxy shields of pests like mealybugs and scale. Evaporates quickly.
