Pothos (Epipremnum aureum): The ultimate water cleaner. Don't submerge the actual plant. Instead, hang the roots directly into the water of the tub and let the leafy vines drape over the outside edges. The roots will form a beautiful underwater jungle that sucks up nitrates like a vacuum, and the fish can't destroy the leaves.

Hornwort (Ceratophyllum demersum): A fast-growing, hardy submerged plant. It doesn't need to be rooted in substrate; you can just let it float. It is excellent at competing with algae for nutrients.

Water Lettuce or Water Hyacinth (Floating): If your setup gets bright ambient light or sunlight, these floating plants have massive, feathery root systems that strip nutrients out of the water instantly. Note: Koi love to nibble on these roots, so you may need to replace them occasionally or partition them off.

Anubias and Java Fern: Tough, leathery-leafed submerged plants. You superglue or tie them to a heavy rock. Koi usually find these too tough to chew on, so they survive the fish's constant foraging.
