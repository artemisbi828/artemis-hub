# Rate vs Proportion
5 girls, 2 boys, 7 total
Proportion: 5/7 (Part / Whole)
Ratio: 5:2 (Part vs Part), 5/2
- no concept of "whole" --> relative size not share of total
- can be greater than 1
Rate: Quantity / Unit (time, distance, population, etc.)
- 5 births per year
- 60 miles per hour
- 3 cases per 1,000 people

| Expression             | Term           | Why             |
| ---------------------- | -------------- | --------------- |
| 5 girls / 7 total      | **Proportion** | Part-to-whole   |
| 2 boys / 7 total       | **Proportion** | Part-to-whole   |
| 5 girls : 2 boys       | **Ratio**      | Part-to-part    |
| Girls per year         | **Rate**       | Involves time   |
| Girls per 100 students | **Rate**       | Different units |
