# Amount x Period Based
**Production Split Method**
Revenue generated in period (eg month)

**Cash Split Method**
Cash collected or received in period (eg month)
# Time Based
**No Lag (Same‑Month Attribution)**
Marketing Spend in Month → NPEs / Case Starts in same month
Simplest but misleading, only if business insists

**Defined Lag Attribution**
Simple: Marketing Spend in Month‑1 → NPEs / Case Starts in Month
Simple, easy to understand

Multi: Marketing Spend in Month‑1 and Month‑2 → Weighted impact on Month outcomes
More realistic, more modeling
