Here’s a **tight, executive‑resonant vocabulary set** you can draw from—terms that signal **brevity, clarity, precision, and impact** without sounding academic or fluffy. I’ve grouped them by _what they emphasize_, because executives react differently depending on context.

---

## 1️⃣ Brevity + Clarity (executive gold)

Use these when you want to signal _“you’ll get this fast.”_

- **Crisp**
- **Succinct**
- **Concise**
- **Tight** (“a tight summary”)
- **Direct**
- **Plain‑spoken**
- **Sharp**
- **Clean**
- **High-signal** (favored by technical execs)
- **No-friction** (modern exec-speak)

✅ Example

> “We’ll provide a **crisp, high‑signal summary** for leadership.”

---

## 2️⃣ High Fidelity / Precision (trust‑building words)

These signal **accuracy, rigor, and lack of distortion**—critical for metrics and governance.

- **High‑fidelity**
- **Faithful** (to source, intent, or definition)
- **Exacting**
- **Precise**
- **Authoritative**
- **Definitive**
- **Ground‑truth aligned**
- **Auditable**
- **Traceable**
- **Non‑ambiguous**

✅ Example

> “This is a **high‑fidelity, traceable definition**—no interpretation required.”

---

## 3️⃣ Resonance / Executive Impact (how it _lands_)

These describe _emotional and cognitive stickiness_ without sounding marketing‑heavy.

- **Resonant**
- **Compelling**
- **Persuasive**
- **Anchoring** (excellent exec term)
- **Stick‑the‑landing**
- **Salient**
- **Intuitive**
- **Outcome‑oriented**
- **Decision‑shaping**
- **Board‑ready**

✅ Example

> “We need an **anchoring narrative** executives can repeat.”

---

## 4️⃣ Signal over Noise (very executive‑friendly)

These imply respect for time and cognitive load.

- **Signal‑dense**
- **Noise‑free**
- **Stripped‑down**
- **Essential**
- **Material**
- **What‑matters**
- **Executive‑filtered**
- **Pre‑digested** (use carefully; slightly informal)

✅ Example

> “This view is **signal‑dense and noise‑free**.”

---

## 5️⃣ Authority without verbosity

These project confidence, not explanation.

- **Declarative**
- **Unqualified** (when appropriate)
- **Bottom‑line**
- **Decisive**
- **Positioned**
- **Settled**
- **Closed‑form** (great for analytics audience)
- **Single‑answer**

✅ Example

> “This is the **closed‑form answer** we’re using.”

---

## 6️⃣ Phrases executives consistently respond to

These test extremely well in leadership rooms:

- **“Executive‑ready”**
- **“At‑a‑glance”**
- **“Single source of truth”** (still powerful when earned)
- **“Bottom‑line up front” (BLUF)** ✅
- **“Decision‑grade”**
- **“No caveats version”**
- **“Short answer” / “Long answer available”**
- **“You don’t need the weeds”**

✅ Example

> “Here’s the **decision‑grade, BLUF version**.”
