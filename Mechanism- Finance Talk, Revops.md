| Accrued Revenue     | Revenue that has been earned but not yet received in cash or recorded. This is often used in accrual accounting.                                 |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Deferred Revenue    | Revenue received in advance for goods or services to be delivered in the future. This is more about revenue already received but not yet earned. |
| Forecasted Revenue  | Projected or expected revenue based on models, historical data, or business plans.                                                               |
| Revenue Pipeline    | Often used in sales and business development to describe expected future revenue from deals in progress.                                         |
| Revenue Projections | Estimates of future revenue, typically used in budgeting or financial planning.                                                                  |
| Expected Revenue    | A general term for anticipated income in a future period.                                                                                        |
