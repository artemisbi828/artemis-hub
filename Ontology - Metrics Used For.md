# Specific Template
### Used To Track

#### Strategic Growth

- New patient growth velocity by period
- Net expansion vs stagnation by location / region
- Contribution of new patients to long‑term production forecasts

#### Demand Generation

- Effectiveness of marketing campaigns at driving _actual_ patient additions
- Channel‑level acquisition performance (online booking vs PSC/NPC vs in‑clinic)
- Marketing spend efficiency when paired with cost data

#### New Business Pipeline

- Top‑of‑funnel health for downstream production and collections
- Conversion of scheduled NPEs into realized patient additions
- Early warning signal for future revenue shortfalls

#### Operational Health

- Clinic capacity alignment relative to incoming new patients
- Detection of operational friction causing dismissals or leakage
- Balance between new and returning patient load

#### Execution Performance

- Team performance in handling NPE flow
- Effectiveness of intake, scheduling, and follow‑up processes
- Impact of operational workflows on net patient conversion

#### Digital & Application Performance

- Online Booking Flow effectiveness
- Drop‑off evaluation between scheduling and completed NPE
- Comparative performance of digital vs assisted scheduling paths

#### Forecasting & Planning

- Leading indicator for future production and collections
- Input to staffing, chair utilization, and clinic expansion planning
- Volume assumptions in short‑ and mid‑term forecasts

#### Financial Impact (Indirect)

- Revenue enablement through downstream production
- ROI assessment when paired with marketing and staffing costs
- Sensitivity analysis for growth‑driven financial outcomes

#### Governance & Accountability

- Performance reviews for clinics, regions, and growth functions
- Incentive alignment for growth‑focused roles
- Objective baseline for growth targets and variance analysis

---

## Documentation Rule (Strongly Recommended)

For every metric:

> **At least one application must live outside the analytics org.**

If a metric cannot be tied to:

- A decision
- An owner
- Or an action

…it is **not production‑ready**, regardless of technical correctness.
