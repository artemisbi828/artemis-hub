### North Carolina–Grounded Tech Staffing Agencies

North Carolina’s Research Triangle (Raleigh-Durham) and Charlotte hubs have strong regional agencies with direct pipelines into enterprise data pipelines, banking data warehouses, and remote-friendly tech teams.

* **KORE1 (Charlotte / Raleigh / Remote):** Specializes heavily in data infrastructure, analytics engineering, cloud data platforms, and data architecture. They place across contract, contract-to-hire, and full-time remote roles.
* **Parallel Partners (Raleigh / RTP):** A boutique IT recruiting agency rooted in the Triangle. They focus heavily on software, systems, and senior data engineering placements with mid-market and high-growth tech firms.
* **Eliassen Group (Charlotte & Raleigh branches):** Known for national technology consulting and agile staffing, Eliassen has dedicated practices for data architecture, modern data stacks (Snowflake, Databricks, dbt), and cloud migrations.
* **Insight Global (Charlotte & Raleigh):** Operates on a scale similar to Robert Half, but with a dedicated national **Big Data & Analytics Practice** handling high volumes of data engineering and cloud architect positions. ^save-point
* **Apex Systems (Raleigh & Charlotte):** A major technical staffing player with deep enterprise client relationships across the Southeast and remote US markets, frequently staffing long-term contract and direct-hire data platform roles.

### California & National Agencies (Data & Cloud Specialists)

West Coast agencies frequently source remote talent across Eastern/Central time zones because it gives California tech teams extended operational runway.

* **Burtch Works (National / Strong West Coast Reach):** Widely recognized as one of the premier recruiting firms dedicated specifically to data science, data engineering, ML, and enterprise data architecture.
* **Motion Recruitment (San Francisco, Silicon Valley, LA / Remote):** Specializes in tech placement with dedicated recruiters by stack (e.g., Python/Spark, Cloud Data Warehouses, Distributed Systems). A large portion of their West Coast client requisitions are open to 100% remote engineers.
* **Redfish Technology (Silicon Valley / Remote):** A long-running boutique tech recruiting firm focused on VC-backed software and data-driven startups. They specialize in early-to-mid stage companies looking for foundational data architects.
* **Averity (National / Remote Practice):** Specializes exclusively in data science, analytics engineering, and cloud data architecture, prioritizing high-touch candidate vetting over mass resume blasting.

### Connecting with Technical Recruiters

1. **Target Specific Practice Leads on LinkedIn:** At large agencies (like Insight Global or Apex), general recruiters may still work generic IT support. Search LinkedIn for titles like *"Technical Recruiter - Data Engineering"* or *"Practice Lead - Data & Analytics"* at these firms.
2. **Highlight Infrastructure Over Modeling:** Data science recruiters often get swamped by modeling/ML applicants. Ensure your headline and opening blurb explicitly lead with infrastructure keywords (e.g., *Data Architect / Staff Data Engineer | Spark, Kafka, Snowflake, Airflow, AWS/GCP*).
3. **State Working Hours Flexibility Upfront:** When pitching California agencies, note that you are based in North Carolina (EST) but comfortable overlapping core Pacific Time (PST) hours. Remote teams in the Bay Area often view East Coast overlap as an asset rather than a blocker.
