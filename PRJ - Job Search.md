# Where to find work
Beyond Upwork and Fiverr, the landscape for analysts and programmers has shifted toward **vetting** and **niche specialization**. Instead of competing in a "race to the bottom" on price, these platforms focus on matching high-quality talent with specific technical needs.

Here are the best alternatives categorized by how they work:

### 1. The "Elite" Platforms (High Pay, High Vetting)

These sites screen you heavily (interviews, coding tests, logic exams) but offer significantly higher hourly rates ($60–$200+/hr) and premium clients like Airbnb or Pfizer.1

- **Toptal:** The most famous "Top 3%" platform.2 They accept developers, data analysts, and finance experts.3 Once you're in, they handle the matching for you.4
    
- **Arc.dev:** Specifically for remote software engineers. They offer a "fast-track" for senior developers to get hired by Silicon Valley startups.
    
- **Andela:** Originally focused on African talent but now global. They specialize in placing senior engineers into long-term roles at enterprise companies.
    

### 2. Niche Tech & Data Job Boards

If you prefer applying to specific roles rather than building a "gig" profile, these are the gold standards:

- **Dice:** The oldest and most reliable tech-only job board in the US. Great for data analysts, DBAs, and software engineers.
    
- **Wellfound (formerly AngelList):** The go-to for **startup work**.5 If you want to be the first analyst or lead programmer at a high-growth company, this is where you look.
    
- **Ottis:** A newer, high-signal platform that uses AI to match your specific tech stack (e.g., Python/Snowflake/dbt) with companies that actually use those tools.
    

### 3. Commission-Free & Modern Freelance

Upwork and Fiverr take a large percentage of your check. These platforms are changing that model:

- **Contra:** A "New Gen" freelance platform that charges **0% commission** to the freelancer.6 It acts more like a professional portfolio where you can also manage contracts and payments.
    
- **Malt:** Very popular in Europe.7 It functions like a high-end search engine for consultants. Clients reach out to you based on your specific expertise (e.g., "Tableau specialist in Berlin").
    
- **Twine:** Excellent for **Data Analysts**.8 They have a specific focus on "Marketing & Product Analytics" and help connect analysts with companies needing cohort analysis or attribution modeling.
    

### 4. Remote-First Specialized Sites

- **We Work Remotely (WWR):** The largest community for remote work.9 They have a massive "Programming" and "Data" section.
    
- **Remote OK:** Great for seeing "salary transparency." Most listings here show the pay range upfront, which saves you from wasting time on low-budget projects.
