# Domains

**Point-of-Care (POC) Media:** The primary domain, focusing on digital out-of-home (DOOH) advertising specifically tailored for clinical environments.

**Patient Engagement & Education:** Providing clinically vetted content via waiting room screens and interactive exam room tablets to drive health literacy and screenings.

**Provider Practice Solutions:** Digital signage and internal communication tools (HCP/Staff meeting room screens) designed to streamline practice operations and physician-staff alignment.

**Data-Driven Patient Targeting:** Utilizing "Precision Patient Journey" analytics to deliver mobile and digital advertisements based on medical claims data and specialty location.

# Lines of Business (LOB)

### 1. Life Sciences & Brand Solutions

The primary revenue engine, connecting brands to patients and HCPs.

* **Waiting Room & Exam Room Media:** Video and interactive ad units placed in high-dwell-time areas.
* **Mobile Targeting:** Geo-fencing and digital extensions that follow the patient before and after their visit.
* **Check-in/Patient Portal:** Digital placements within the administrative workflow of the patient visit.

### 2. Provider Solutions (Clinical & Operational)

Value-add services for healthcare practices and hospital systems.

* **Patient Education:** Content libraries integrated into exam room screens to assist HCPs in explaining complex diagnoses.
* **Staff Engagement:** Private screens for breakrooms/meeting rooms to disseminate practice-wide announcements and training.
* **Clinical Outcomes Programs:** Specialized digital campaigns focused on increasing screenings (e.g., mammograms, colonoscopies) and vaccination rates.

### 3. Data & Insights

The "Intelligence" layer of the platform.

* **Matched-Pair Analysis:** Measuring the "lift" in prescriptions or screenings by comparing offices with PatientPoint versus those without.
* **Patient Journey Mapping:** Leveraging medical claims data to optimize ad placement within specific specialties (Oncology, Cardiology, etc.).

# Strategic BI Observations

### 4.1 Competitive Positioning

PatientPoint holds a dominant position in the POC space following its merger with Outcome Health. Its "moat" is physical: once hardware is installed in a doctor's office, the switching costs are high. The platform benefits from a dual-sided value proposition: providers receive "free" or subsidized technology, while brands gain access to a highly captive, high-intent audience that is increasingly difficult to reach via traditional TV or digital web ads.

### 4.2 Growth Vectors

* **Telehealth & Digital Portals:** Expanding beyond the physical office into the virtual care journey to capture remote patient interactions.
* **Specialty Expansion:** Increasing footprint in high-value therapeutic areas like Oncology and Neurology where high-cost specialty drugs require intensive patient education.
* **HCP Marketing:** Growing the "Staff Meeting Room" segment to influence physician prescribing behavior directly, rather than just patient request behavior.

### 4.3 Clarifications & Ambiguities

1. **Ad-Revenue Concentration:** The specific percentage of revenue derived from top-10 pharmaceutical firms is not public, though likely represents a significant majority.
2. **Provider Cost Structure:** It is unclear which portions of the digital hardware are provided at zero cost to the physician versus those that require a monthly service fee.
3. **Data Privacy Impact:** Shifts in healthcare data privacy regulations (HIPAA/state-level) could impact the granularity of their "Precision Patient Journey" targeting.
