# Domains
HealthTech / Digital Health: Their primary industry, focused on providing technology solutions for healthcare settings.

Point-of-Care Marketing (POC): This is the specific sub-niche they lead. It refers to marketing that reaches patients while they are receiving care.

AdTech (Advertising Technology): Because they manage a programmatic digital out-of-home (DOOH) network with specific targeting and attribution capabilities.

Patient Engagement Solutions: Their functional category within the healthcare provider workspace.

# Lines of Business (LOB)
### 1. Marketing & Advertising (Point-of-Care)

PatientPoint acts as a specialized advertising network for pharmaceutical, OTC, and consumer health brands.

* **Precision Targeting:** They allow brands to reach patients at various stages of the "patient journey"—pre-visit (mobile), during the visit (waiting room/exam room screens), and post-visit.
* **Contextual Relevance:** Unlike broad digital ads, their marketing is highly contextual; ads for specific medications or health products are shown in relevant specialist offices (e.g., oncology, dermatology, or cardiology).
* **Behavioral Change:** The goal is to drive specific "point of change" actions, such as asking a doctor about a specific brand, increasing first-fill rates, or improving medication adherence.

### 2. Account-Based Management (ABM) for Brands

In the pharmaceutical and life sciences world, PatientPoint functions as an ABM tool by enabling "hyper-local" targeting:

* **HCP Targeting (Healthcare Professional):** They offer dedicated screens in **HCP/Staff Meeting Rooms**. This allows brands to run "account-based" messaging directly to doctors and clinical staff throughout their workday, educating them on new clinical data or treatment protocols.
* **Practice-Level Customization:** They help pharmaceutical sales reps support specific "high-value accounts" (medical practices) by providing those practices with digital signage that helps the office run more efficiently while simultaneously displaying the brand’s messaging.
* **Data-Driven ROI:** They use medical claims data and matched-pair analysis to prove to their brand partners that their presence in specific "accounts" (doctor offices) directly correlates to an increase in prescriptions or screenings.

### 3. Provider Engagement (The "B2B" Side)

For the healthcare providers (the "accounts" they manage), PatientPoint provides a suite of tools to improve practice efficiency:

* **Patient Education:** Automated content in exam rooms to save doctors time on basic explanations.
* **Practice Promotion:** Tools for practices to market their own ancillary services (like flu shots or aesthetics) to their existing patient base.
* **Staff Communication:** Back-office screens to streamline internal communications without using email.
