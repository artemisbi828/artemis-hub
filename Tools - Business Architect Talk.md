# Engagement Types
embedded == company laptop; service-account; domain-member; custom solutions
injected == setup low-footprint satellite; nightly or 2x day dataloads to system
bridged == manual drag-n-drop
hosted == client; pay a subscription/retainer; they use services; start-stop == on-demand;

# Example
website-design:
- embedded -- I am an employee, they pay me + give me an expense card -- setup everything upon approval, create service-accounts; do docmentation; leave -- significant commitment; best for custom;
- injected -- low footprint local app; auto-bridge w/ data needed; setup service-account (or mine)
- hosted -- I setup everything; they pay a flat fee; start-stop on demand -- lowest cost; template + pay extra for custom enhancements; limited scope (convert to embedded if very custom); they tell me what they want, I do it for them;
- bridged -- similar to hosted; but very low; their employee; notification for misses;
