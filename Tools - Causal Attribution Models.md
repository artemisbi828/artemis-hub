**Simple Lag Attribution**
Marketing Spend in Month‑1 → NPEs / Case Starts in Month
Simple, easy to understand

**Multi‑Lag Attribution**
Marketing Spend in Month‑1 and Month‑2 → Weighted impact on Month outcomes
More realistic, more modeling

**No Lag (Same‑Month Attribution)**
Marketing Spend in Month → NPEs / Case Starts in same month
Simplest but misleading, only if business insists
