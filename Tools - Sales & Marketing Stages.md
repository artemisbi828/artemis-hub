### 1. The "Transaction" Survey (The Post-Purchase Stage)

Sent immediately after a sale or a specific support interaction.

- **Metric:** **CSAT** (Customer Satisfaction Score).
- **Question:** "How would you rate your experience today?"

### 2. The "Health Check" Survey (The Retention Stage)

Sent periodically (every 3–6 months) to ensure the customer isn't planning to leave.

- **Metric:** **NPS** (Net Promoter Score).
- **Question:** "How likely are you to recommend us to a friend?"

### 3. The "Effort" Survey (The Product/Service Stage)

Sent after a user tries a new feature or navigates your dashboard.

- **Metric:** **CES** (Customer Effort Score).
- **Question:** "How easy was it to resolve your issue today?"

> **Pro Tip:** If a customer gives you a high score in the **Retention** stage, immediately move them to the **Advocacy** stage by asking for a LinkedIn testimonial or a referral!
