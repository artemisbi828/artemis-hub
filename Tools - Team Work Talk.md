A polished PMO (Project Management Office) phrase for "spread too thin" could be:
"Overextended across multiple initiatives"

Other professional alternatives include:
	• "Resource bandwidth is constrained"
	• "Operating at capacity across concurrent priorities"
	• "Limited availability due to competing demands"
	• "Allocation dilution across strategic efforts"
	• "Insufficient resourcing to sustain current workload"
