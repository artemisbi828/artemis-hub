## Core equivalents to *complete thinking*

These all share the sense of **wanting the whole picture before acting**.

- **Global thinking** ✅ (your preferred)
- **Totalizing thinking** (slightly critical; implies swallowing everything into one frame)
- **Holistic thinking** (emphasizes wholes over parts; softer, almost therapeutic)
- **Integrative thinking** (focuses on reconciling pieces into one schema)
- **Comprehensive thinking** (coverage-oriented; nothing left out)
- **System-level thinking** (explicitly steps above local instances)

## Words that emphasize *closure* rather than scale

These highlight the *need to finish*, resolve, or settle understanding.

- **Closed-form thinking**
- **Settled thinking**
- **Resolved thinking**
- **Conclusive thinking**
- **Finalized thinking**
- **End-state thinking**

These contrast nicely with fractal thinking, which is *open* by default.

---

## Words that emphasize *structure and coherence*

These care less about scale and more about **internal consistency**.

- **Architectural thinking**
- **Coherent thinking**
- **Unified thinking**
- **Monolithic thinking** (usually critical)
- **Framework-driven thinking**
- **Schema-first thinking**

Useful if the tension you’re naming is *“I need a frame before I move.”*

## Words that emphasize *abstraction*

These sit closer to the danger zone you were pointing at earlier.

- **Abstract-first thinking**
- **Model-driven thinking**
- **Theory-complete thinking**
- **Conceptual closure**
- **Generalizing thinking**
- **Pattern-final thinking**

These are often where hasty abstraction sneaks in.
