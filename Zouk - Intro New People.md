# Background
Jonas | Brazilian Zouk & Partner Dance Specialist

With 15 years on the dance floor, Jonas’s journey is a roadmap of rhythm—stretching from the soulful streets of Detroit to the heart of Des Moines and the vibrant energy of Austin.

While his roots began in Bachata, his true passion lies in the flow and connection of Brazilian Zouk. To master the craft, Jonas traveled directly to the source, immersing himself in intensive training in both Rio de Janeiro and São Paulo.

The Philosophy: Dance as Dialogue

Jonas believes that while many can master choreography, true partner dancing is an art of communication, not memorization. He teaches that a dance is a living conversation—a constant cycle of calling, answering, waiting, and listening.

 * Mind Over Movement: Jonas prioritizes the "mental game" of connection over the rigid memorization of patterns.

 * The "Mistake" Perspective: In Jonas’s classroom, there are no mistakes—only "new possibilities." He encourages dancers to lean into the organic, the natural, and the unexpected.

 * Hip Hop Influence: Drawing from his love of Hip Hop, he brings a deep focus on emoting through movement, helping students find their own voice within the structure of a partner dance.

> "Partner dance isn't about two people performing solo moves in proximity; it’s an organic dialogue. It’s about being present enough to hear what your partner is saying without words."

# What to Expect in Class

Whether you are a seasoned lead/follow or just starting your Zouk journey, Jonas focuses on breaking the habit of "rote dancing." You’ll learn to move with intention, embrace the flow of the moment, and turn every "wrong" step into a beautiful new direction.
